Behind the archive

Created by Aaron

The Revealed Cosmos is an independent speculative-fiction universe written and directed by Aaron. It began as a Russian-language story archive and is now expanding in English through cinematic images, original music and compact visual narratives.

The work centers on vivid worlds, unusual systems and the human pressure inside them. Each release reveals one fragment while preserving the mystery of the larger whole.
